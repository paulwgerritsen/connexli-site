1 · Why compare agents at all?

Would you accept the first quote for anything else this big?

Most people spend more time comparing airfares than comparing the person who will help them make the largest purchase of their life. That's not carelessness — until recently, there was no practical way to compare buyer's agents at all. You met one through a friend or an open house, and that was that.

But agents genuinely differ: in what they charge, in how fast they respond, in how well they know Lehi versus Ogden, in whether they've closed three buyer deals in your price range this year or thirty. Choosing an agent is a decision, not a default.

The sealed-proposal difference: on Connexli, agents can't see each other's offers. That means each one brings you their best terms first — not the minimum needed to edge out a rival.

2 · The rules changed in 2024

Buyer representation isn't what it was when your parents bought

In 2024, a landmark legal settlement changed how buyer's agents work in the United States. Three changes matter to you:

  • Written agreements come first. Before touring homes with an agent, you'll now sign a written agreement (often called a Buyer Broker Agreement) spelling out what they'll do and what they'll charge.
  • Fees are specific and negotiable. The old assumption — "the seller just pays it, don't worry about it" — is gone. Your agent's compensation is stated up front, and it's negotiable, which is exactly why comparing matters.
  • Read before you sign. That agreement is a real contract. Signing one with the first agent you meet, before comparing anyone, is how buyers end up locked in — which is precisely what Connexli exists to prevent.

None of this is bad news. It's transparency — and transparency rewards buyers who compare.

3 · The question everyone's silently asking

What does a buyer's agent actually cost?

Honest answer: it varies — and since 2024, it varies more than ever. That's the point of comparing. The structures you'll see in Connexli proposals:

  • Percentage at closing — a percent of the purchase price, paid when you close.
  • Flat fee — one stated dollar amount, regardless of price.

One more thing worth knowing: sellers may contribute toward your agent's fee as part of the deal, and every Connexli proposal must state plainly how a shortfall is handled — either you cover the difference, or the agent accepts the seller-paid amount subject to a stated minimum fee.

Why compare if the seller may contribute? Because the amount you agree to with your agent is your contractual responsibility, and a seller's contribution may not cover all of it. A simple illustration — example only, not a recommended rate: a buyer agrees in writing that their agent will be compensated $15,000. If the seller or listing side ultimately contributes $12,000 toward that compensation, the buyer could be responsible for the remaining $3,000 under the buyer's agreement. Actual obligations depend on the contracts and transaction.

Compare before you commit. Connexli gives buyers the opportunity to understand what different professionals propose to charge and what they provide in return — before selecting representation. And the lowest number isn't automatically the best: compare compensation together with services, experience, responsiveness, negotiation approach, and fit.

What we won't tell you: that you're required to have an agent (you're not), or that Connexli guarantees savings (no honest platform can). What we will do is show you real numbers, side by side, before you commit to anyone. You'll know what you'd pay before you ever share your name.

4 · The part that surprises people

Agents compete for you without knowing who you are

The number-one reason people won't fill out real estate forms: the phone starts ringing and never stops. Connexli was built specifically to kill that experience.

  • Agents see an anonymous snapshot of your search — areas, price range, timeline, priorities. Never your name, email, or phone.
  • No agent can call, email, or message you while you compare. There is nothing to cold-call.
  • When you choose, your contact info goes to that one agent only. The others never learn who you were.
  • You can walk away at any point owing nothing, and every agent on the platform is license-verified before they can send a single proposal.
